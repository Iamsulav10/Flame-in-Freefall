## Repository and rights policy

PSI entry point: https://psi.nasa.gov/physci/repo/ . Repository citation guidance asks users to cite DOI/persistent identifiers. PSI source records above list CC0-1.0. Publication rights remain distinct.

NASA media guidance: https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/ . No NASA logos, mission emblems, photographs or copied PDFs are included; no endorsement is implied.

Original source records are cited even when freely available. Supporting paper abstracts are paraphrased with identifiers and locators. The source license field distinguishes verified public-use statements from unknown redistribution terms.
